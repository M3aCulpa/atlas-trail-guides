Indoor Herb Gardening: A Complete Guide

Growing herbs indoors has never been easier with modern LED grow lights and self-watering planters. This guide covers everything from seed selection to harvest.

Growing Basil Indoors

Basil thrives in warm conditions with at least 6 hours of light daily. Use a south-facing window or supplement with a full-spectrum LED grow light positioned 6-12 inches above the plants.

Mint Varieties

Peppermint and spearmint are the most popular choices for indoor growing. Keep mint in its own container as it spreads aggressively and will overtake other herbs.

Harvesting Tips

Regular harvesting encourages bushier growth. Cut stems just above a leaf node, taking no more than one-third of the plant at a time.
