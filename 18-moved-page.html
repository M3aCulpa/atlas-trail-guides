Major Landslide Closes Elk Ridge Trail System Indefinitely; Three Hikers Rescued

By Sarah Whitfield | Published October 3, 2024 | Updated October 4, 2024

Originally published at: https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?article=elk-ridge-landslide&edition=2024-10-03

A massive landslide triggered by heavy overnight rainfall has closed the entire Elk Ridge Trail system in Cascade National Forest, county officials confirmed Thursday morning. The slide, estimated at 200,000 cubic yards of debris, buried approximately 1.2 miles of the popular loop trail between mile markers 4 and 5.2.

Three hikers who had camped overnight at the Ridge Top backcountry site were cut off when the slide blocked the only maintained trail back to the trailhead. A Cascade County Search and Rescue helicopter extracted all three shortly after 9 a.m. None required medical attention.

"The scale of this slide is significant," said Cascade County Emergency Manager Tom Reeves at a press briefing Thursday afternoon.
