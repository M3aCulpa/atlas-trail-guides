Understanding Home Network Security

Your home network is the gateway to all your connected devices. Here is how to secure it properly.

Router Configuration

Change the default admin password on your router immediately after setup. Use WPA3 encryption if your devices support it, WPA2 at minimum. Disable WPS as it has known vulnerabilities.

Network Segmentation

Create a separate guest network for visitors and IoT devices. This prevents a compromised smart device from accessing your computers and phones on the main network.

DNS Security

Consider using DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) or DNS-over-TLS (DoT) to encrypt your DNS queries. Services like Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Quad9 (9.9.9.9) offer encrypted DNS with malware blocking.

Firmware Updates

Enable automatic firmware updates on your router. Manufacturers regularly patch security vulnerabilities. If your router no longer receives updates, it is time to replace it.

Monitoring

Periodically review connected devices through your router's admin panel. Unknown devices could indicate unauthorized access to your network.
