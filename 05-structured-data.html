Cloudpeak Trail Runner X3: Full Review

After 200 miles on a mix of technical single-track and fire roads, the Cloudpeak Trail Runner X3 has exceeded expectations in nearly every category.

Outsole Performance

The Vibram Megagrip compound paired with 5mm lugs provides exceptional traction on wet rock and loose gravel. The lug pattern channels mud effectively, preventing buildup during sloppy conditions.

Midsole Comfort

Cloudpeak's proprietary ReactiveFoam delivers a 32mm stack height with a 6mm heel-to-toe drop. It absorbs impact on descents without feeling mushy on climbs.

Upper and Fit

The engineered mesh upper breathes well on warm days and dries quickly after stream crossings. The toe bumper is substantial enough to handle rock strikes without being heavy.

Verdict

At $160, the X3 competes favorably with the Salomon Speedcross and Hoka Speedgoat. If you prioritize grip and durability over weight savings, this is the trail shoe to beat in 2026.
