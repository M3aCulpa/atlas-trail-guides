Top 10 Coffee Brewing Methods Ranked

Coffee enthusiasts have long debated the best way to brew the perfect cup. We surveyed 500 baristas worldwide to settle the debate once and for all.

1. Pour Over (V60)

The pour over method remains the gold standard for extracting nuanced flavors from single-origin beans. The Hario V60 leads the category with its spiral ridges that promote even extraction.

2. AeroPress

Loved by travelers and home brewers alike, the AeroPress offers remarkable versatility. Its pressure-based extraction produces a clean cup with low acidity.

3. French Press

The classic immersion method delivers a full-bodied cup with rich oils. Four minutes of steeping at 200F produces optimal results.

4. Espresso

True espresso requires 9 bars of pressure and finely ground coffee. Modern machines from brands like La Marzocca and Decent Espresso have made cafe-quality shots achievable at home.

The remaining methods, including cold brew, moka pot, siphon, Turkish, Chemex, and drip, each have dedicated followings for good reason.
