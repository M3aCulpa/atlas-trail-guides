Weekly Meal Prep Ideas for Busy Professionals

Spending 2-3 hours on Sunday preparing meals for the week can save you significant time and money. Here are five meal prep strategies that scale well.

1. Grain Bowl Assembly Line

Cook a large batch of quinoa or brown rice. Prepare 3-4 protein options (grilled chicken, baked tofu, hard-boiled eggs, canned chickpeas). Chop vegetables and store in separate containers. Assemble each morning in under 2 minutes.

2. Sheet Pan Dinners

Season different proteins and vegetables on sheet pans. Roast at 425F for 25-30 minutes. Each pan feeds 3-4 meals. Favorites include salmon with asparagus, chicken thighs with sweet potatoes, and sausage with peppers.

3. Freezer-Friendly Soups

Make a double batch of soup and freeze individual portions. Lentil soup, chicken tortilla soup, and minestrone all freeze exceptionally well. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat at lunch.

4. Overnight Oats

Combine rolled oats, milk (or plant milk), chia seeds, and your preferred sweetener in mason jars. Prepare five at once for grab-and-go breakfasts all week.

5. Snack Prep

Portion out trail mix, cut vegetables with hummus, and prepare energy balls. Having healthy snacks ready reduces the temptation of vending machines and delivery apps.
