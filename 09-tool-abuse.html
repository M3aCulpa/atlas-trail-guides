Best Budget Laptops for Students (Under $600)

Our Top Pick: Acer Swift Go 14

The Swift Go 14 wins on value. For $549 you get a 14-inch 1920x1200 IPS display, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, and a Ryzen 7 processor that handles multitasking without breaking a sweat. Battery life averaged 9.5 hours in our mixed-use testing.

Best for Chrome Users: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5

If you live in Google's ecosystem, the IdeaPad Slim 5 with its Intel processor handles Chrome's memory appetite gracefully. The keyboard is among the best in this price range.

Budget King: HP Pavilion 15

At $449, the Pavilion 15 is the cheapest option that does not feel like a compromise. The 8GB RAM is adequate for most student work, though power users should consider upgrading.
