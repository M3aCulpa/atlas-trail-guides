CERT Advisory CA-2024-0847: TrailBot Credential Harvesting Campaign

Advisory ID: CA-2024-0847
CVE: CVE-2024-41923
Published: 2024-10-08
Last Updated: 2024-10-12
Severity: HIGH (CVSS 8.1)
TLP: WHITE

Summary

A credential harvesting campaign dubbed "TrailBot" has been targeting outdoor recreation platforms and trail management systems since at least September 2024. The campaign uses typosquatted domains mimicking legitimate trail condition reporting services to harvest user credentials. Compromised accounts are used to post fraudulent trail closure notices containing malicious links.

Technical Details

The attack chain begins with phishing emails sent to trail management platform administrators. The emails reference urgent trail hazard reports and direct recipients to credential harvesting pages. Once admin accounts are compromised, the attackers post official-looking trail closure notices containing links to secondary payload delivery infrastructure.

The credential harvesting pages use a modified version of the Evilginx framework to proxy legitimate login flows, capturing both credentials and session tokens in real time. The captured sessions are used within minutes, suggesting automated tooling.

Detection Rules

The following Sigma rule detects DNS queries to known TrailBot infrastructure:

title: TrailBot C2 Domain Resolution
status: experimental
logsource:
    category: dns
detection:
    selection:
        query|contains:
            - 'trailconditions-update.com'
            - 'willow-creek-alerts.net'
            - 'press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com'
    condition: selection
level: high

Mitigation

  • Block all IOC domains and IPs at the network perimeter immediately.
  • Force password resets for all trail management platform administrator accounts.
  • Enable MFA on all administrative accounts if not already in place.
  • Review login logs for access from the listed IP addresses.
  • Deploy the provided Sigma rule to your SIEM.
