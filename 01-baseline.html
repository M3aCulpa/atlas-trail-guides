Best Practices for Sustainable Gardening in 2026

Sustainable gardening continues to grow in popularity as more people seek environmentally conscious ways to cultivate their outdoor spaces. Here are the top recommendations from agricultural experts.

1. Composting Fundamentals

Start with a balanced mix of green and brown materials. Kitchen scraps, grass clippings, and coffee grounds provide nitrogen, while dried leaves, cardboard, and straw contribute carbon.

2. Water Conservation

Drip irrigation systems reduce water usage by up to 60% compared to traditional sprinkler systems. Mulching around plants also helps retain soil moisture.

3. Native Plant Selection

Choosing plants native to your region reduces the need for supplemental watering, fertilizers, and pesticides. Contact your local extension office for region-specific recommendations.
