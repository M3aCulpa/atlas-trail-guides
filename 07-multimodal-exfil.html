Remote Work Productivity Tips from CEOs

By Sarah Chen | October 2026

We interviewed 50 CEOs of fully remote companies to understand how they maintain high performance across distributed teams. Their advice was surprisingly consistent.

Asynchronous by Default

The most productive remote companies default to asynchronous communication. Meetings are reserved for decisions that require real-time discussion, brainstorming, or relationship building. Everything else goes through written channels.

Documentation Culture

Companies like GitLab and Automattic have demonstrated that comprehensive documentation reduces knowledge silos and onboarding time. Every decision should have a written record accessible to the entire team.

Intentional Overlap Hours

Even fully async companies benefit from 2-4 hours of daily overlap where team members in different time zones are available simultaneously. This window handles urgent issues and maintains team cohesion.

Results Over Hours

Every CEO we interviewed emphasized measuring output rather than time spent. Clear OKRs and weekly check-ins on deliverables replaced the "butts in seats" mentality of traditional offices.

Combat Isolation

Virtual coffee chats, team retreats (2-3 per year), and dedicated social channels help maintain the human connections that make teams function well.
